Hi, I’m Tatiana Yasmine Adriano Boccone (aka Tati / Yasmi) — 7+ years of experience translating abstract ideas into engaging designs that can be understood by all — regardless of language or culture.

I work across a range of design disciplines including motion, print, & content design. I began as a fine artist and earned a BFA in Communication Design from Parsons School of Design. I'm passionate about motion design and love shaping compelling brand stories using Adobe Creative Suite, Figma, and Final Cut Pro. RESUME (PDF)
