Hi, I’m Tatiana Yasmine Adriano Boccone (aka Tati / Yasmi) — Born + raised in Manila, now living in Manhattan. 7+ years of experience translating abstract ideas into engaging designs that can be understood by all — regardless of language or culture.

I work across a range of design disciplines including motion, print, & content design. I began as a fine artist and went on to earn a BFA in Communication Design from Parsons School of Design. I'm passionate about motion design and love shaping compelling brand stories using tools like Adobe Creative Suite, Figma <3 (aka love of my life), and Final Cut Pro. RESUME (PDF)

As Marketing Manager at TRESI, I filmed, edited, and produced the entry video featured on the company’s website.
Visualising data and memory (archive) without language is a theme I explored during my Thesis earning my BFA in Parsons School of Design.“Beyond the Cover” is a visual self-portrait in book form, tracing my journey as a half-Filipino, half-American navigating cultural displacement and identity. Through fragmented portraits, landscapes, and still lifes, the work reflects my evolution from a state of unbelonging to a loving acceptance of self.
